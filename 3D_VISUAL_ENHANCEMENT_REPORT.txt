SKY CADASTRAL - 3D REAL-ESTATE MASTER PLAN VISUAL ENHANCEMENT REPORT
================================================================================

1. VISUAL CHANGES MADE
----------------------
- Transformed the basic Three.js viewer into a professional real-estate master plan and architectural land development visualization.
- Created a natural, cohesive site plan atmosphere featuring realistic ground terrain, development asphalt roads, concrete boundary curbs, lane dash markings, a grand entrance gate monument, low-poly 3D tree species, street lamp posts, and park amenities.
- Refined the 2D CAD vector view with road dash lines, entrance banner, park trees, and a North compass rose, ensuring full visual and architectural alignment between 2D and 3D views.

2. 3D RENDERING CHANGES
-----------------------
- Upgraded the Three.js rendering pipeline in PlotViewer3D with optimized geometry extrusion, capped pixel ratio scaling (1-2x), and frustum culling.
- Fixed 2D-to-3D axis mapping in geometryUtils.js so extruded plot geometry, ground elements, floating badges, street lights, and camera target points align 100% accurately on the positive Z plane (0 to 160).
- Applied polygon offsetting and edge wireframe line segments for sharp CAD boundary rendering.

3. MATERIALS
------------
- Plot Materials:
  * Available: Natural emerald/teal land material (#10b981), low elevation (0.3 units), crisp boundary trim.
  * Booked: Muted amber land material (#d97706), reduced saturation and 0.65 opacity.
  * Sold: Neutral matte slate grey material (#475569), low visual prominence.
  * Selected: Animated vertical lift (+0.35 units), cyan boundary highlight (#0284c7), soft outer boundary glow without harsh neon effects.
- Road Materials: Deep development asphalt (#262930, roughness 0.85) with concrete curbs (#94a3b8) and white/yellow lane markings.
- Landscaping Materials: Realistic meadow grass turf (#1e3422), stone pillars (#334155), warm timber pergola wood (#78350f), gravel paths (#d97706).

4. LIGHTING
-----------
- Warm 5600K directional sunlight (#fffbeb, intensity 1.6) with 2048x2048 soft shadow maps.
- Hemisphere sky/ground light (#bfdbfe sky, #1c3320 ground) providing natural daylight fill illumination.
- Subtle point lights on street lamp fixtures (#fef08a, distance 12) for architectural detail.
- Atmospheric horizon fog (#0b1329) blending the terrain seamlessly into the background.

5. TERRAIN
----------
- Created SubtleTerrain.jsx using custom plane geometry with procedural elevation contours around the outer landscape perimeters.
- Kept the master plan plot area flat and stable while surrounding it with low rolling meadow hills.
- Placed plots naturally on the ground substrate rather than floating.

6. ROADS

- Added concrete boundary curb edges running along road perimeters.
- Added dashed center lane markings along the 60ft Boulevard and Entrance Way.
